About me

I'm Marlen, a Lead Product Designer at Avonova. I lead a team of product designers and work hands-on in a cross-functional product team building digital products for HR and health and safety.

My background is in traditional UX design, covering everything from user research and early concepts to prototyping, testing and developer handoff. Since June 2026, we've been moving towards a more AI-driven way of working. Today, I work more as a full-stack designer, using Claude Code to design, build and test ideas directly in code.

I'm also responsible for our component library, building and maintaining reusable components in collaboration with other designers. This helps us create consistent experiences while making it easier to move from ideas to working products.

Before moving into product design, I spent many years working with branding and marketing at OBOS and Avonova. That experience still shapes how I think about design. I care about creating products that are easy to use, brands that feel consistent across every touchpoint, and solutions that are built to last.
