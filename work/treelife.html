Treelife

Website

UI design · UX design · Web design

Overview

TreeLife wants to give back to nature by getting people to plant more trees to fight climate change. Trees take carbon dioxide out of the air we breathe.

Many of TreeLife's customers care about nature and the climate. They see the value of planting trees, but may not know how, or have the space to do it themselves.

Project brief

Design a four-page website for TreeLife's initiative to educate people about tree planting.

  • Give users information about how to choose the most suitable tree for the space available
  • Explain how to plant a tree
  • Provide the option to donate a tree
  • Design these pages: Home, About, Contact
