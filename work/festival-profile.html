Rodi Festival

2014 · Illustration · Graphic design

In this school project I made up a festival concept. The theme is a travelling people in Norway who spoke a secret language called Rodi. Inspired by this, I created a "secret" festival that moves from place to place on a low budget. To show that the festival moves around, I chose a caravan as the logo.
