SolarSmart

Brand, style guide, website and design system

Visual identity · Style guide · Website · Design system

Overview

SolarSmart wants to protect the planet for the generations to come. They believe that using renewable energy in daily life can make the world better.

They also want more people to understand why electricity should come from renewable energy.

Project brief

SolarSmart want to develop a profile including a new logo, a brand style guide, a website and a design system. It needs to contain rules about:

  • Brand personality
  • Tone of voice
  • A logo
  • Colour palette
  • Typography
  • Imagery
  • Icons and buttons
Roles
  • User Experience (UX) Designer
  • User Interface (UI) Designer
Deliverables
  • Low-fidelity wireframes
  • High-fidelity mockups and prototypes
  • Style guide
  • Design system
  • Specification
Duration
  • 4 weeks
Tools
  • Figma, Photoshop
